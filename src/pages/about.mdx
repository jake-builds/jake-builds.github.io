## How I Approach the Work

Start with a concrete problem. Build something small enough to try. Use AI to help with exploration and implementation, while keeping responsibility for the decisions and the result.

My current experiments bring together modern web development, local-first product ideas, prompt design, and AI-assisted workflows. I share the public parts here without exposing private work or data.
